## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-precision, workstation-grade environment built for enthusiast hardware control, performance benchmarking, and telemetry interfaces. It synthesizes technical minimalism with controlled cybernetic luminosity—eschewing chaotic gamer tropes in favor of an elite, architectural aesthetic tailored for pro-tier power users, audio engineers, and hardware overclockers.

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
- **Precision Engineered:** Every micro-interaction, container border, and typographic alignment suggests aerospace machining and rigorous calibration.
- **Controlled Kinetic Energy:** Electric sky and cyan glows are treated as targeted instrumentation cues rather than decorative sprawl. Ambient energy only pulses to validate active states, thermal thresholds, and hardware telemetry.
- **Architectural Immersion:** Deep obsidian and slate tiers establish immediate visual hierarchy, allowing telemetry data, charts, and hardware states to emerge with absolute optical clarity.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Tonal Minimalist Architecture** and **Precision Glassmorphism**. UI elements leverage deep multi-tiered dark surfaces, sharp 1px structural ghost borders with adaptive opacity, and focused cyan/electric sky emissive highlights. Surfaces avoid flat pure blacks, relying instead on graded midnight slate tones to sustain continuous spatial depth.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an obsidian substrate accented by surgical, cold-spectrum photonics.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base (`#090D16`):** The foundational substrate representing zero-plane space. Used for application-level root viewports.
- **Surface Low (`#0D1322`):** Primary structural zones, persistent sidebars, and structural utility panels.
- **Surface Container (`#131B2E`):** Standard modular cards, telemetry monitors, and elevated modules.
- **Surface High (`#1A233A`):** Interactive inputs, elevated modal windows, hover targets, and flyouts.
- **Surface Stroke (`#232F4C`):** Subdued ghost borders providing crisp demarcation without visual clutter.

### Accent & Emissive Palette
- **Electric Sky Primary (`#38BDF8`):** Applied to primary actions, active telemetry lines, selected radio states, and critical metric badges.
- **Electric Indigo Secondary (`#6366F1`):** Applied to secondary interactive surfaces, grouped category indicators, and supporting data visualizations.
- **Hyper Cyan Tertiary (`#00E5FF`):** Reserved for live status indicators, maximum performance profiles, overclocks, and low-latency feedback.

### Typography & Content Color System
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`):** Pure contrast level for headlines, key parameters, and primary actions.
- **Text Muted (`#94A3B8`):** Structural labels, metadata, secondary readouts, and unselected tab headers.
- **Text Dim (`#475569`):** Disabled states, fine-print telemetry units, and subtle watermarks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends geometric presence with technical clarity.

- **Display & Headlines (`Sora`):** Chosen for its technological curvature and structured weight distribution. Used exclusively for brand anchors, high-level device categorizations, performance modes, and card headers. Sora imparts a futuristic, machined poise.
- **Body & Controls (`Geist`):** Selected for its hyper-rational legibility, dense horizontal metrics, and developer-grade rendering fidelity. It ensures data tables, diagnostic streams, configuration lists, and tooltips remain sharp and fatigue-free under extended operational cycles.

### Typographic Implementation Rules
- Keep `label-md` and `label-lg` uppercase when applied to metric tags, device slots, and hardware status pins.
- Use tabular figures for numeric metrics (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`) across all real-time readouts (e.g., frequencies, wattages, temperatures).
- Avoid letter spacing greater than `0.06em` to maintain a crisp, industrial profile.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an adaptable 12-column grid anchored by a strict 4px base increment. Spacing creates dense, information-rich interfaces without visual crowding.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout. Margin of `2rem` (`space-xl`), standard gutters at `1.5rem` (`gutter`). Enables multi-pane workstation views: sidebar navigation (span 2), primary telemetry visualization (span 7), hardware control rack (span 3).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Margins collapse to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), gutters reduce to `1rem` (`gutter-sm`). Secondary controls fold into collapsible horizontal drawer segments.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout. Margins scale down to `1rem` (`margin-sm`), gutters lock at `1rem` (`gutter-sm`). Data readouts stack vertically with horizontal swipe-accessible control carousels.

### Spacing Usage
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-gaps between telemetry values and unit descriptors; internal icon-to-label spacing inside compact chips.
- `space-sm` (8px): Gaps between list rows, input internal padding (vertical), and metric badge clusters.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal container padding for compact telemetry cards, control group separation.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard modular card padding and major section separation within panels.
- `space-xl` (32px): Structural canvas canvas-level offsets and primary section separators.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via tonal layering paired with controlled emissive photonics, avoiding heavy, traditional drop shadows in favor of precise spatial stacking.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid `#090D16`. Ground layer.
2. **Tier 1 (Panels & Docks):** `#0D1322` with a 1px border of `#1A233A`. Non-interactive structural zones.
3. **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules):** `#131B2E` with a 1px border of `#232F4C`. Standard hoverable/tappable card states.
4. **Tier 3 (Modals & Flyouts):** `#1A233A` with a 1px border of `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)` and a subtle ambient occlusion shadow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`).

### Emissive Accents & Glow States
Interactive nodes employ low-spread ambient glows to signal engagement:
- **Active State Glow:** `box-shadow: 0 0 16px -2px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`
- **Extreme Threshold / Overclock State:** `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.45)`
- **Glass Shroud (Overlays):** `backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background-color: rgba(9, 13, 22, 0.85)`

## Shapes

The design system employs **Roundedness 2**, establishing an engineered balance between razor-sharp technical precision and modern ergonomics.

### Corner Radii Applications
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Standard buttons, input fields, dropdown trigger targets, toggle tracks, and chip badges.
- **Large Surfaces (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px):** Modular cards, telemetry clusters, nested viewports, and floating tool palettes.
- **Structural Containers (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px):** Primary dialogs, full-scale modal windows, and application frame boundaries.
- **Micro Radii (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Progress bar fills, custom scrollbar thumbs, and status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#38BDF8` background with `#090D16` typography (Sora, 600 weight). On hover: brightness increases to `#00E5FF` accompanied by a localized cyan aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 14px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.4)`). Height: 40px. Padding: 0 20px.
- **Secondary:** Surface background `#131B2E` with 1px border `#232F4C` and `#F8FAFC` text. On hover: border transitions to `#38BDF8` with a 10% sky tint overlay.
- **Ghost / Utility:** Transparent fill, `#94A3B8` icon/label. On hover: background shifts to `#1A233A` with `#F8FAFC` text.

### Inputs & Sliders
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#0D1322`, border 1px `#232F4C`, text `#F8FAFC`. Focus state creates a 1px border transition to `#38BDF8` along with a subtle focus glow (`0 0 0 1px #38BDF8`). Placeholder text locked to `#475569`.
- **Hardware Sliders:** Track height 4px in `#1A233A`. Active track fill in gradient from `#6366F1` to `#38BDF8`. Thumb is an 18px circle (`#F8FAFC`) with an internal core of `#090D16` and an electric sky perimeter halo on interaction.

### Cards & Modules
- Structured on `#131B2E` with 1px ghost border `#232F4C`.
- Card headers utilize `Sora` headline-md with uppercase tracking.
- Cards feature optional top-edge glowing accent lines (1px height gradient from transparent to `#38BDF8` to transparent) to denote primary status or active telemetry monitoring.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Height 24px, radius 8px. Surface `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.1)`, text `#38BDF8`, border 1px `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`.
- **System Tags:** Solid `#0D1322` fill, text `#94A3B8`, border 1px `#1A233A`.

### Lists & Data Rows
- Row items separated by 1px subtle divider `rgba(35, 47, 76, 0.5)`.
- Hover triggers background transition to `#1A233A` with a left border highlight indicator (2px solid `#38BDF8`).

### Checkboxes & Toggle Switches
- **Toggle Switches:** Track 44px x 24px in `#1A233A`. Active track transitions to `#38BDF8`. Thumb: 18px pure white circle with 2px offset.
- **Checkboxes:** 18px x 18px square with 4px radius. Border 1px `#232F4C`. Checked state fills `#38BDF8` with `#090D16` check icon.

### Diagnostic Gauges & Telemetry Panels (Specialized)
- Radial or horizontal bar visualizers built using SVG layers.
- Underflow/safe ranges in `#38BDF8`, performance tiers in `#6366F1`, and peak overclock/thermal limits in `#00E5FF`.
- Gauge track background remains strictly `#0D1322`.
